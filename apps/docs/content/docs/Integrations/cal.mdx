Integrate **Datamate** with your **Cal.com** scheduling pages to gain insights into user behavior and improve your booking performance.

> **TL;DR:** Connect your Cal.com account to Datamate using your Client ID.

---

## How to set up Datamate with Cal.com

1. Go to your [Datamate dashboard](https://app.datamate.cc) and copy your **Client ID**.
2. Log in to your [Cal.com account](https://app.cal.com).
3. Open the [**Datamate App**](https://app.cal.com/apps/datamate) in the App Store.
4. Click **Install App**.
5. Activate it on each event page you want to track by adding your **Client ID**.
   > For self-hosted setups, you can also provide your **API URL** and **Script URL** [see details](/docs/Integrations/cal#for-self-hosted-datamate-users).

That's it! Your Cal.com pages are now connected to Datamate.

---

## Security settings

By default, Datamate only accepts requests from your website's registered domain. To track analytics from Cal.com booking pages, you need to add Cal.com to your allowed origins.

**Go to:** Open [Websites](https://app.datamate.cc/websites), select your site, then go to **Settings → Security**.

**Add these origins:**

```
cal.com
*.cal.com
```

<Callout type="info">
  The wildcard `*.cal.com` covers all Cal.com subdomains including `app.cal.com` and any custom subdomains.
</Callout>

Learn more about [security settings](/docs/security#access-control-settings).

---

## For self-hosted Datamate users

If you're using a self-hosted version of Datamate:

1. Enter your **Client ID**, **API URL**, and **Script URL** during setup.
2. Save and complete the installation.

---

## Use case

You can use the **same Client ID** across your **main website** and **Cal.com booking pages** to track full user journeys.

Example:

- A visitor lands on `yourwebsite.com/pricing` → clicks "Book a demo" → schedules via your Cal.com page.
- In Datamate, you can use the [dashboard](/docs/dashboard) to measure drop-off, set up goals, and optimize conversions.
